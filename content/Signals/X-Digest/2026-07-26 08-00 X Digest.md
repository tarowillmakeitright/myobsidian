## 1. Japan’s extreme-heat streak reaches a record fifth day

## 2. EU AI transparency rules enter application on August 2

**Why it matters:** Providers and deployers in scope must disclose AI interactions and label or machine-mark certain AI-generated material, including deepfakes and some public-interest content. Penalties can reach €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover. **Action:** teams serving EU users should review Article 50 coverage, labeling, provenance metadata, and user notices now.

**Source:** [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/guidelines-transparency-obligations-providers-and-deployers-ai-systems)
