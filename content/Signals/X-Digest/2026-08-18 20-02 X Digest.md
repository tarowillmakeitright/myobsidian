**Why it matters:** WMO puts the likelihood of El Niño developing during June–August at 80%, rising above 90% for persistence through at least November. Governments, agriculture operators, insurers, and supply-chain teams should review heat, drought, flood, and wildfire contingencies now.

**Source:** [United Nations / WMO summary](https://www.un.org/en/property-cards-by-og-global-category/287/6979)
