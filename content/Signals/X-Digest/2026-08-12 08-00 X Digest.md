## 1. U.S. CPI lands tonight — the clearest near-term macro catalyst

**Why it matters:** July CPI is due at 21:30 JST (08:30 ET). June CPI was 3.5% year-over-year while energy prices were unusually volatile, so the print can quickly move rate expectations, the dollar, bonds, equities, crypto, and USD/JPY. **Action:** avoid oversized short-term positions into the release; watch headline CPI, core CPI, and revisions rather than reacting to the first number alone.

**Source:** [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — CPI](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/)

## 2. Microsoft’s August Patch Tuesday is a high-priority admin cycle

**Why it matters:** Security communities are actively triaging a large August update set, including discussion of a critical HTTP.sys issue affecting an older Windows 10 branch. Microsoft has also warned that AI-assisted bug discovery can make monthly patch volumes larger and delivery patterns less uniform. **Action:** review the Microsoft Security Update Guide, test the cumulative update on a small ring, then prioritize internet-facing Windows and Exchange systems. Treat community CVE counts as provisional until your inventory is matched against Microsoft’s data.

**Sources:** [Microsoft Security Update Guide](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/), [Microsoft Security Response Center — note on Patch Tuesday](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/msrc/blog/2026/05/a-note-on-patch-tuesday), [r/sysadmin Patch Tuesday megathread](https://www.reddit.com/r/sysadmin/comments/1vliawb/patch_tuesday_megathread_august_11_2026/)
