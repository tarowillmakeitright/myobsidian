# X Digest — 2026-06-24 20:02 JST

## 1) SpaceX to buy Cursor maker Anysphere in a $60B all-stock deal
**Why it matters:** This is one of the clearest signs that AI coding tools have moved from hype to strategic infrastructure. A deal at this size says enterprises now see coding copilots/agents as core productivity and platform assets, not side tools.
**Source:** https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/spacex-buy-anysphere-60-billion-2026-06-16/

## 2) The Fed turned more hawkish, with nearly half of policymakers now seeing a 2026 rate hike
**Why it matters:** This is a major macro signal because higher-for-longer or renewed hikes tighten financial conditions, lift the dollar, pressure risk assets, and ripple into global equities, bonds, and emerging markets.
**Source:** https://www.reuters.com/business/nearly-half-fed-policymakers-see-2026-rate-hike-cards-2026-06-17/

## 3) Bank of Japan lifted rates to 1%, a 31-year high
**Why it matters:** BOJ normalization is no longer theoretical. It matters for the yen, Japanese equities, global carry trades, and bond markets. If Japan keeps tightening while the yen stays weak, intervention and volatility stay on the table.
**Source:** https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/global-markets-view-usa-2026-06-19/

## 4) Japan is reportedly targeting 370 trillion yen in public-private investment by 2040
**Why it matters:** The scale is huge, and the focus areas — AI, chips, and space — line up with sectors already attracting global capital and policy support. If this gets implemented seriously, it could materially shape Japan’s industrial strategy and investment flows.
**Source:** https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/japan-target-23-trillion-public-private-investment-by-2040-nikkei-reports-2026-06-20/

**Why it matters:** This remains an important cross-market driver because any disruption or normalization around Hormuz quickly feeds into oil, inflation expectations, central-bank pricing, and geopolitical risk sentiment.
