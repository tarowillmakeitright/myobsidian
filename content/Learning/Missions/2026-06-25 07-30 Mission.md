# Mission: Track the ISS Over Tokyo

**Objective:** Learn how orbital data becomes a practical prediction by finding the next ISS passes over Tokyo and explaining what the numbers mean.

## Steps
1. Open Heavens-Above or NASA Spot The Station and set location to Tokyo.
2. Find the next 2 visible ISS passes.
3. Record for each pass: date/time, max elevation, direction of appearance, and duration.
4. Read a short explainer on what TLE/orbital prediction data is.
5. Write a 5-7 sentence summary: why passes change daily, what max elevation means, and what conditions make a pass visible.
6. If possible, add one screenshot of the pass chart/map.

## Expected Output Artifact
A short Obsidian note with:
- a table of the next 2 ISS passes over Tokyo
- a brief explanation of TLE/orbital prediction concepts in your own words
- one screenshot or linked source

## Stretch Challenge
Compare the ISS prediction from 2 different sources and note one difference in timing or visibility, plus your guess why.
