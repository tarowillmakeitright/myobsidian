# Edge AI Mission: Benchmark a Tiny Local Embedding Workflow

#learning #mission #ai #devops #science [[Home]]

## Objective
Build a tiny local text-embedding benchmark and compare at least 2 short inputs for speed and similarity behavior. Finish with one short note on where this could be useful.

## Steps
1. Create a small test set of 6 sentences: 3 about DevOps, 3 about space/science.
2. Use a local or lightweight embedding tool/library you already have, or install one if needed.
3. Generate embeddings for all 6 sentences.
4. Measure total runtime and per-item runtime.
5. Compute cosine similarity for at least 3 pairs:
   - two related DevOps sentences
   - two related science sentences
   - one cross-topic pair
6. Record which pair was most similar and whether that matched your intuition.
7. Write a 5-7 line summary with one practical use case.

## Expected Output Artifact
A single note or script containing:
- the 6 sample sentences
- runtime numbers
- the 3 similarity scores
- a short conclusion

## Stretch Challenge
Repeat the benchmark with a second embedding model or different sentence wording and compare how the rankings change.
