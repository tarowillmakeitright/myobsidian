## Mission Title
Build a Tiny RAG Evaluator with Local Notes

## Objective
In 30–60 minutes, create a minimal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflow and measure whether retrieval actually improves answer quality on 5 test questions.

## Exact Steps
1. Create a folder `rag-mini/` with three files: `notes.md`, `questions.md`, `eval.md`.
2. Put ~8–12 short facts in `notes.md` (mix AI, DevOps, and space topics).
3. Write 5 questions in `questions.md` that require those facts.
4. For each question, answer once **without retrieval** (from model only), then answer again **with retrieval** by copying the top relevant note snippets.
5. In `eval.md`, score both answers (0–2) for factual correctness and briefly explain differences.
6. Summarize: “When did retrieval help most, and why?” in 3 bullet points.

## Expected Output Artifact
A completed `eval.md` containing:
- 5 question pairs (no-retrieval vs retrieval answers)
- Per-question scores
- A short conclusion with 3 practical takeaways

## Stretch Challenge
Add one “distractor” note with misleading but plausible info, then update your retrieval prompt/rules to reduce wrong-context usage.
