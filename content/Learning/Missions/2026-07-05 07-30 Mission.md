## Mission Title
Build a Tiny RAG Evaluator

## Objective
Learn how retrieval quality affects AI answers by comparing 3 chunking strategies on the same small document set.

## Exact Steps
1. Pick 3-5 short documents you already have locally (notes, articles, or markdown files).
2. Create three chunking versions:
   - fixed 300-character chunks
   - fixed 800-character chunks
   - paragraph-based chunks
3. For each version, run the same 3 questions against your retriever or simple embedding search setup.
4. Record for each question:
   - top retrieved chunk
   - whether it actually contained the answer
   - whether the final answer was correct
5. Write a short comparison: which chunking style gave the best retrieval precision and why.

## Expected Output Artifact
A markdown note or CSV table with 9 total test results (3 chunking styles × 3 questions) plus a 5-sentence conclusion.

## Stretch Challenge
Add one reranking step or metadata filter, then check whether it improves at least 2 of the 9 results.
